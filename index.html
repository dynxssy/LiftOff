Install

  1. Move Liftoff to ApplicationsUnzip the download and drag the app into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it onceControl-click Liftoff and choose Open. If macOS blocks it, use Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security.
  3. Choose how to open itThe short setup lets you pick F4, a trackpad pinch or a hot corner, and imports your old Launchpad layout.
